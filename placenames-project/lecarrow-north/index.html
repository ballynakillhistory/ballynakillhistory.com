Lecarrow North

Lecarrow North in Irish is “An Leath Cheathrú Thuaidh”, meaning half quarter north. In the Electoral division of Marblehill the townland is 118.78 acres or 48.07 hectares in size.

In the 1841 census there were 9 people and 1 house, and in the 1851 census there were 6 people and 1 house. The 1840 map showed 2 houses or outbuildings, and records a ringfort in the north of the townland; the nearby residence, still occupied today, is known as Fort Lodge. Griffith’s Valuation recorded 2 houses and 2 landholders, and in both the 1901 and 1911 censuses there was 1 residence and 5 inhabitants. Logainm records the townland as Lecarrow McDermotroe in the 17th century.

Lecarrow North borders Ballycorban, Cuilleendaeagh, Gortnakilla, Knockadrum and Lecarrow South.
