Derryvunlam

Derryvunlam in Irish “Doire Bhunlam” is 1174.27 acres or 475.21 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Abbeyville.

Derryvunlam borders Ballygowan, Bauntia, Cappagh, Clonco, Cloonmoylan, Drumminnamuckla South, Easterfield, Lackan, Rossmore and Rosturra.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 481 people and 83 houses in Derryvunlam, and by the 1851 census there were 361 people and 65 houses. In the 1901 census there were 106 people and 18 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 97 people and 19 houses.

Landholders and heads of household recorded in the Tithe Applotment Books, Griffith’s Valuation, and the 1901, 1911 and 1926 censuses.
