Knockdrummore

Knockdrumore in Irish “Cnoc Droma Móir” meaning big ridge hill or hill of the great ridge. In the Electoral division of Marblehill it is 54.05 acres or 21.87 hectares in size.

In the 1841 census there were 16 people and 3 houses there and in the 1851 census there were 18 people and 2 houses. The 1840 map shows 3 houses or outhouses a substansial police barracks. In the 1847 -64 Griffiths Evaluation there was no houses, 2 landholders and a Police Barracks now known as Cartron House. There are no returns for the 1901 and 1911 census.

In the south of the townland there is a ringfort and an area known as Terry Hill possibly connected with an agrarian revolt conducted by a group known as the ‘Terry Alts’ 1829-31.
