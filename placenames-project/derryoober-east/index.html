Derryoober East

Derryoober East in Irish “Doire Úbair Thoir” is 538.75 acres or 218.03 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drummin.

Derryoober East borders Coos North, Derryoober West and Gorteeny.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 89 people and 16 houses in Derryoober East, and by the 1851 census there were 45 people and 7 houses. In the 1901 census there were 73 people and 11 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 62 people and 10 houses.
