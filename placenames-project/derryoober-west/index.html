Derryoober West

Derryoober West in Irish “Doire Úbair Thiar” is 1187.25 acres or 480.46 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Coos.

Derryoober West borders Alleendarra East, Coos North, Derrygill, Derrygoolin North, Derryoober East, Gorteeny and Ulicks Mountain.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 56 people and 7 houses in Derryoober West, and by the 1851 census there were 83 people and 10 houses. In the 1901 census there were 9 people and 2 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 20 people and 2 houses.
