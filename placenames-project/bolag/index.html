Bolag

Bolag in Irish “Both Leag” is 433.32 acres or 175.36 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Woodford.

Bolag borders Clonco, Derrycrag, Drumminnamuckla South, Laughil and Woodford.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 203 people and 39 houses in Bolag, and by the 1851 census there were 84 people and 14 houses. In the 1901 census there were 82 people and 18 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 46 people and 13 houses.

Land and Household Records

Landholders and heads of household recorded in the Tithe Applotment Books, Griffith’s Valuation, and the 1901, 1911 and 1926 censuses.
