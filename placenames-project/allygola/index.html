Allygola

Allygola in Irish “Aill an Ghualaigh” meaning the cliff of charcoal is 212.06 acres or 85.82 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drumkeary.

Allygola borders Barratoor, Burroge, Callatra, Derrygarriff, Larraga, Liss and Parkagarraun.

About the Townland

The landlords of Allygola were the Burkes of Marble Hill. The charcoal for which the townland is named was usually made from local hardwood and used as fuel for blacksmiths in the area, and in the 1800s Allygola had a water-powered corn mill.
