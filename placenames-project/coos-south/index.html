Coos South

Coos South in Irish “An Cuas Theas” is 979.01 acres or 396.19 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Coos.

Coos South borders Coos North and Reynclamper.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 441 people and 68 houses in Coos South, and by the 1851 census there were 316 people and 59 houses. In the 1901 census there were 138 people and 28 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 130 people and 27 houses.
