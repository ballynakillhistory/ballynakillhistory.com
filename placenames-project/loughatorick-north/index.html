Loughatorick North

Loughatorick North in Irish “Loch An Tóraic Thuaidh” is 3194.75 acres or 1292.87 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Loughatorick.

Loughatorick North borders Derreennamucka, Laggoo, Loughatorick South, Moyglass and Toorleitra.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 178 people and 28 houses in Loughatorick North, and by the 1851 census there were 97 people and 18 houses. In the 1901 census there were 118 people and 22 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 100 people and 21 houses.
