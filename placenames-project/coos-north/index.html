Coos North

Coos North in Irish “An Cuas Thuaidh” is 1216.36 acres or 492.24 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Coos.

Coos North borders Coos South, Derrygoolin North, Derryoober East, Derryoober West, Gorteeny and Reynclamper.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 317 people and 57 houses in Coos North, and by the 1851 census there were 185 people and 43 houses. In the 1901 census there were 112 people and 21 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 102 people and 19 houses.

Land and Household Records

Landholders and heads of household recorded in the Tithe Applotment Books, Griffith’s Valuation, and the 1901, 1911 and 1926 censuses.
