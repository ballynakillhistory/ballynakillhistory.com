Curragh

Curragh in Irish “An Currach” is 163.04 acres or 65.98 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drumkeary.

Curragh borders Carrowcrin, Derrygarriff, Lisheeny, Liss, Parkagarraun, Tomany Beg, Tomany More and Tonroe.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 80 people and 11 houses in Curragh, and by the 1851 census there were 72 people and 8 houses. In the 1901 census there were 43 people and 9 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 31 people and 9 houses.
