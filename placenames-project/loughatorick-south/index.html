Loughatorick South

Loughatorick South in Irish “Loch An Tóraic Theas” is 2952.73 acres or 1194.93 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Loughatorick.

Loughatorick South borders Derrygoolin North, Loughatorick North and Toorleitra.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 219 people and 41 houses in Loughatorick South, and by the 1851 census there were 156 people and 24 houses. In the 1901 census there were 125 people and 20 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 126 people and 18 houses.
