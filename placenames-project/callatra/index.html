Callatra

Callatra in Irish “Calataire” meaning a place abounding in hazel according to John O’Donovan is 121.97 acres or 49.36 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drumkeary.

In the 1841 census there were 76 people and 14 houses and in the 1851 census the population had fallen to 50 people and 9 houses. The landlords of Callatra were the Burkes of Marble Hill.

Callatra borders Allygola, Barratoor, Derrygarriff and Tonroe.
