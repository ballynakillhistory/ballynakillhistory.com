Knockauncarragh

Knockauncarragh in Irish “An Cnocán Carrach” is 729.99 acres or 295.42 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Woodford.

Knockauncarragh borders Alleendarra East, Alleendarra West, Commons East, Drumminnamuckla North, Laughil and Woodford.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 191 people and 33 houses in Knockauncarragh, and by the 1851 census there were 96 people and 17 houses. In the 1901 census there were 48 people and 10 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 65 people and 13 houses.
