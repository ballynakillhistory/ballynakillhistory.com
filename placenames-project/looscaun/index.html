Looscaun

Looscaun in Irish “An Luascán” is 362.88 acres or 146.85 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drummin.

Looscaun borders Clonco, Derrygill, Drummin, Oghilly, Rossmore and Rostollus.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 189 people and 32 houses in Looscaun, and by the 1851 census there were 105 people and 21 houses. In the 1901 census there were 70 people and 12 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 60 people and 11 houses.
