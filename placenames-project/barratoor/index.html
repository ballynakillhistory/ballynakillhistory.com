Barratoor

Barratoor in Irish “Barr an Tuair” meaning the top of the bleach or green field according to John O’Donovan is 263.97 acres or 106.82 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drumkeary.

Barratoor borders Allygola, Callatra, Drumkeary East, Kyleaglannawood, Larraga, Ross, Tonroe and Tooreen South.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 183 people and 29 houses and in the 1851 census the population had fallen to 148 people and 27 houses. There are approximately 21 houses in Barratoor at the time of writing (2026).

Land and Household Records

Landholders and heads of household recorded in the Tithe Applotment Books, Griffith’s Valuation, and the 1901, 1911 and 1926 censuses.
