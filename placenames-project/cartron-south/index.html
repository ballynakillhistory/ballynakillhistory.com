Cartron South

Cartron South in Irish “An Cartrún Theas” meaning quarter of land south. In the Electoral division of Marblehill it is 70.48 acres or 28.52 hectares in size.

In the 1841 census there were 11 people and 1 house and there were no returns for the 1851 census. The 1840 map shows 2 houses or outbuildings. In the 1847 -64 Griffith’s Valuation there was no house and 1 landholder. In the 1901 census there is 1 residence and 9 inhabitants and in the 1911 census there is 1 residence and 6 inhabitants.

There is a ringfort situated in the south of the townland and towards the centre on an impressive elevated site is a monument classified as a radial stone cairn, reputed to be the only one in Co. Galway. These cairns found mainly in Cork and Kerry are rare and are thought to date from the Bronze Age.

Cartron South borders Ballycorban, Ballynakill, Knockadrum, Knockdrummore and Loughpark.
