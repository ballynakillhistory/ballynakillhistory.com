Knockaundarragh

Knockaundarragh in Irish “Cnocán Darach” meaning oak hillock according to Logainm is 328.28 acres or 132.85 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Marblehill.

In the 1841 census there were 26 people and 4 houses and in the 1851 census there were 30 people and 5 houses. In 1901 there was a carpenter named Murphy and a tailor named Spain living in Knockaundarragh.

Knockaundarragh borders Allykeolaun, Cullenagh, Drum and Drumkeary West.
