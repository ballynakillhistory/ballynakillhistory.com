Gorteeny

Gorteeny in Irish “Goirtíní” is 1070.91 acres or 433.38 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drummin.

Gorteeny borders Boleynanollag, Coos North, Derrygill, Derryoober East, Derryoober West, Dooros and Oghilly.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 436 people and 69 houses in Gorteeny, and by the 1851 census there were 369 people and 67 houses. In the 1901 census there were 189 people and 33 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 176 people and 30 houses.
