Placenames Project

The Ballynakill Placenames Project is an ongoing project to record the placenames of Ballynakill Civil Parish, with a particular focus on field names and other local names that are often missing from modern maps. As well as placenames, we are recording houses, old family names and other interesting information connected with the townlands.

The project is based largely on information provided by local people, alongside historical maps and other sources. More than 125 people have contributed so far, and we are incredibly grateful to everyone who has shared their time and knowledge with us.

The initial focus of the project was Ballinakill Church Parish but it has since expanded with similar work is also being carried out in Woodford by another group.
