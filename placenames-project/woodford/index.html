Woodford

Woodford in Irish “An Ghráig” is 654.02 acres or 264.67 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Woodford.

Woodford borders Alleendarra East, Bolag, Derrycrag, Derrygill, Knockauncarragh, Laughil and Ulicks Mountain.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 793 people and 154 houses in Woodford, and by the 1851 census there were 637 people and 110 houses. In the 1901 census there were 193 people and 41 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 92 people and 23 houses.
