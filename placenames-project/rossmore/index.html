Rossmore

Rossmore in Irish “Ros Mór” is 1016.50 acres or 411.36 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drummin.

Rossmore borders Bauntia, Clonco, Cloonmoylan, Cloonoon, Derryvunlam, Dooros, Gut Island, Kylenamelly, Looscaun, Rostollus and Srah.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 374 people and 59 houses in Rossmore, and by the 1851 census there were 279 people and 43 houses. In the 1901 census there were 131 people and 23 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 122 people and 20 houses.
