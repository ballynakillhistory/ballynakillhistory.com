Cloonmoylan

Cloonmoylan in Irish “Cluain Maoláin Mór” is 1119.34 acres or 452.98 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Abbeyville.

Cloonmoylan borders Bauntia, Clonco, Derryvunlam and Rossmore.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 338 people and 56 houses in Cloonmoylan, and by the 1851 census there were 267 people and 53 houses. In the 1901 census there were 191 people and 47 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 159 people and 38 houses.
