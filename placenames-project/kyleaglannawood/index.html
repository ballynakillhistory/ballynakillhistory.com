Kyleaglannawood

Kyleaglannawood in Irish “Coill an Ghleanna” meaning wood of the valley according to John O’Donovan is 166.63 acres or 67.43 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Drumkeary.

Kyleaglannawood borders Barratoor, Inchy, Ross, Tooreen North and Tooreen South.

About the Townland

In the mid 1800s the landlord of this townland was Sir Thomas J. Burke of Marble Hill. In the early 1900s McAinsh operated a sawmill there using a light rail system to handle the large hardwoods that grew there. Kyleaglannawood is no longer inhabited and is now mostly a coniferous wood with a small percentage of native hardwoods. There is a good walking route around the wood with the road looping north and south of the Ross river.
