Commons East

Commons East in Irish “An Coimín Thoir” is 430.73 acres or 174.31 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Ballynagar.

Commons East borders Alleendarra West, Ballydaly, Cappaghcon, Cappaghcon East, Cappaghcon West, Commons West, Drumminnamuckla North, Knockauncarragh, Laughil and Lecarrow South.

Population

In the 1841 census there were 23 people and 4 houses in Commons East, and by the 1851 census there were 25 people and 4 houses. In the 1901 census there were 37 people and 8 houses, and in the 1911 census there were 21 people and 6 houses.
