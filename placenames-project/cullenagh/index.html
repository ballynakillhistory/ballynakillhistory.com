Cullenagh

Cullenagh in Irish “Cuileannach” meaning holly land is 664.62 acres or 268.96 hectares in size and is in the Electoral division of Marblehill.

In the 1841 census there were 81 people, 16 inhabited and 1 uninhabited house, and in the 1851 census, after the famine, the population had fallen to 69 people and 13 houses.

A possible standing stone, a large upright slab of limestone about 1.5 metres high, is recorded in the bogland in the south of the townland.

Cullenagh borders Drum, Drumkeary West, Knockaundarragh, Reynabrone, Tooreen North and Tooreen South.
